Runtime converts ARPAbet to broad US IPA. Lexical stress is not scored.
Multiple dictionary pronunciations are displayed and accepted as experimental
comparison variants, without contextual disambiguation. Unknown words are not
assigned a guessed pronunciation score. Existing curated lesson targets take priority.
